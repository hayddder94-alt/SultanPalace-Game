# Character concept sheets

**What these are:** 2D reference images, generated to the locks in
`docs/CHARACTER_BIBLE.md` and the period rules in `docs/WORLD_DESIGN.md` §9.

**What these are not:** 3D models. Nothing here can be imported into Unreal as
a character.

---

## Why they exist

An image-to-3D generator is only as good as the image you feed it. Feed it a
dramatic three-quarter hero shot and you get a lump with a face on one side.
The inputs that actually work are boring on purpose:

| Requirement | Why |
|---|---|
| Straight-on front view, dead centre | the generator infers depth from symmetry |
| A-pose, arms 45° down and away, palms back | limbs must not touch the torso or each other |
| Fingers separated | fused fingers become one mitten of geometry |
| Flat even front lighting | a shadow is read as shape and gets modelled in |
| Plain uniform background | anything busy leaks into the mesh |
| Whole body, nothing cropped | a cropped foot is a missing foot |
| No cape across the body, no hood | occlusion becomes a hole |

Every sheet in this folder is built to those rules.

## The period rules they follow

From `docs/WORLD_DESIGN.md` §9 — Neo-Babylonian, 6th century BCE:

* Bronze, leather, linen, wool. **No plate armour, no chainmail.**
* No European medieval design, no crenellations.
* No Islamic or Ottoman dress, no Ottoman interiors.
* No generic "Arabian Nights" costume from the wrong millennium.
* No 1:1 copy of a museum piece.
