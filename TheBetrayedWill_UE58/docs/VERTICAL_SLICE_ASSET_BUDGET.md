## 11. Placeholder policy

Greybox and mannequins are legal in Phase 9.  
Phase 10 (the gate) requires final-quality **feel + lighting + two-to-four hero faces**, not every dress piece.  
A temp that is illegal to show in a trailer cannot ship in the VS build we photograph.
